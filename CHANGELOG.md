# What's New in KraaFo

---

## July 2026

### Recycle Bin
Deleted invoices, receipts, quotes, and clients now go to a recycle bin instead of disappearing permanently. Restore anything with one click, or empty the bin when you're ready.

### One-Tap Invoice to Receipt
Mark an invoice as fully paid and it converts to a receipt instantly - no re-entering details.

### Shareable Invoice Links
Every invoice now has a hosted link your client can open in any browser directly from WhatsApp or SMS - no app required.

### SMS Delivery
Send documents via SMS alongside WhatsApp and Email. All three channels in one tap.

---

## June 2026

### Ratings & Feedback
Leave a star rating and comment directly on the landing page. Your feedback shapes what gets built next.

### Newsletter & Updates
Subscribe to product updates and get notified when new features ship. Unsubscribe any time with one click from any email.

### Mobile PDF Sharing
On iOS and Android, downloading a PDF now opens the native share sheet - save to Files, send via WhatsApp, AirDrop, and more.

## Launch - June 2026

KraaFo is live. Create professional invoices, receipts, and quotes in under a minute.

- **Invoice, Receipt & Quote builder** - line items, discounts, tax, due dates, notes, and payment terms
- **Live PDF preview** - see your branded document update as you type
- **AI Smart Fill** - suggests line items and payment terms based on your industry and client type
- **Document import** - upload an existing invoice or receipt and the AI reads it and fills the form
- **Triple-channel delivery** - send to a client on WhatsApp, SMS, and Email simultaneously in one tap
- **Client address book** - save and reuse client details across documents
- **Custom branding** - upload your logo, set your colours; every PDF reflects your brand
- **Signature support** - draw or upload a signature; it appears on all generated PDFs
- **Payment details** - add bank account, PayPal, MTN MoMo, M-Pesa, Airtel Money, and more
- **Multi-currency** - USD, GBP, EUR, GHS, NGN, ZAR, and more
- **Multi-device** - your data lives on the server; sign in from any browser or device
