# Software Requirements Specification (SRS)
## KraaFo - Get-Paid Platform for African Small Businesses

**Version:** 1.0 · **Date:** July 2026 · **Status:** Draft for review

---

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Purpose
This document defines the requirements for transforming KraaFo from a collection of features (invoice generator + delivery) into a complete product: an end-to-end system that helps small businesses **create, deliver, track, and get paid for** professional invoices, quotes, and receipts.

### 1.2 The product thesis (why this SRS exists)
The current build solves "make a professional PDF and send it." That is a **feature**. The customer's actual job-to-be-done is **"get paid, without looking unprofessional or chasing clients manually."** A product owns the whole job:

> **Create → Deliver → Track → Remind → Get Paid → Record (receipt) → Repeat**

Every requirement in this document exists to close that loop. Anything that does not serve the loop is out of scope.

### 1.3 Scope
- **In scope:** Web application (mobile-first), document engine (invoice/quote/receipt), branding engine, multi-channel delivery (email, WhatsApp, SMS), hosted document pages, view tracking, payment reminders, payment recording, receipts, user accounts and history, marketing site (SEO), admin console.
- **Phase-2 scope (specified, not built first):** Payment collection (mobile money, bank, card), quote→invoice conversion automation, recurring invoices, team/multi-business support.
- **Out of scope (v1):** Full accounting, inventory, payroll, expense tracking, native mobile apps.

### 1.4 Definitions
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Document | An invoice, quote, or receipt generated by the system |
| Sender | The KraaFo user (business owner/freelancer) issuing a document |
| Recipient | The sender's client who receives the document |
| Hosted page | Public, unique URL where a recipient views a document (kraafo.com/i/{id}) |
| Delivery bundle | One send action that fans out to email + WhatsApp + SMS |
| The loop | Create → deliver → track → remind → paid → receipt |

### 1.5 Intended audience
Founders, developers, designers, and future contributors/investors. This SRS is the single source of truth for what KraaFo is.

## 2. Overall Description

### 2.1 Product vision
**"The fastest way for an African small business to look professional and get paid - on the channels their clients actually use."**

### 2.2 Target users (personas)
1. **The Freelancer (primary)** - designer, developer, consultant, tutor. Bills 2–10 clients/month. Phone-first. Currently invoices via Word docs or WhatsApp text messages. Pain: looking unprofessional, forgetting to follow up.
2. **The Service SME (primary)** - salon, mechanic, events company, NGO/foundation, agency. Bills 10–50 clients/month. Pain: no paper trail, clients "forget" to pay, receipts requested for records/tax.
3. **The Accountant/Bookkeeper (multiplier)** - manages billing for many small clients. Not the v1 build target, but the #1 partnership channel. Requirements must not block a future multi-business mode.

### 2.3 Jobs-to-be-done
| # | Job | Current KraaFo | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| J1 | Look professional to my client | ✅ Branded PDF, logo/color extraction | Copy bugs undermine it |
| J2 | Deliver where my client actually is | ✅ Email + WhatsApp + SMS | Messages are dead ends (no link) |
| J3 | Know if the client saw it | ❌ | Hosted page + view tracking |
| J4 | Chase payment without awkwardness | ❌ | Automated/1-tap reminders |
| J5 | Actually receive the money | ❌ | Phase 2: payment links |
| J6 | Keep records / issue receipts | ⚠️ Receipt exists as a format | Auto-receipt on payment; history |
| J7 | Do it again next month in seconds | ⚠️ Account exists | Saved clients, duplicate invoice, templates |

### 2.4 Operating constraints
- Mobile-first: majority of users on phones with intermittent connectivity.
- Cost-sensitive delivery: SMS and WhatsApp sends have per-message cost; must be metered and abuse-proof.
- Deliverability is existential: the product *is* messaging; sender reputation must be protected (see NFR-SEC and NFR-DEL).
- Free tier must remain genuinely useful; monetization attaches to payments (Phase 2), not to gating basics.

## 3. Functional Requirements

Requirements use MoSCoW priority: **[M]** Must (v1), **[S]** Should (v1 if possible), **[C]** Could (later), **[P2]** Phase 2.

### 3.1 Module: Accounts & Access (AUTH)
- **FR-AUTH-1 [M]** Visitors can create, preview, and download any document **without an account**.
- **FR-AUTH-2 [M]** Sending a document via any channel requires a **verified email account** (verification link or OTP).
- **FR-AUTH-3 [M]** Signup captures: email, password (or magic link), business name. Nothing more at signup; profile completed progressively.
- **FR-AUTH-4 [M]** The signup prompt appears **at the send step**, framed as benefit: "Create a free account to send - your details and history are saved."
- **FR-AUTH-5 [S]** Google sign-in.
- **FR-AUTH-6 [C]** Phone-number OTP login (matches phone-first users).

### 3.2 Module: Business Profile (PROF)
- **FR-PROF-1 [M]** One business profile per account: name, logo, address/country, email, phone, website, default currency, default payment instructions (bank / mobile money details), default payment terms.
- **FR-PROF-2 [M]** Profile data auto-fills every new document ("never retype your info").
- **FR-PROF-3 [S]** Saved client list: recipients are stored on first use and selectable thereafter.
- **FR-PROF-4 [P2]** Multiple businesses per account (accountant mode).

### 3.3 Module: Document Builder (DOC)
- **FR-DOC-1 [M]** Create three document types: **Invoice, Quote, Receipt**, sharing one builder with type-specific fields and wording.
- **FR-DOC-2 [M]** Fields: document number (auto-incremented per business, editable), issue date, due date, line items (description, qty, unit label, unit price, amount), subtotal, tax (inclusive/exclusive, per-line or global), discount, total, amount paid / balance (invoice), notes, terms.
- **FR-DOC-3 [M]** **Due date auto-calculates from payment terms** (Net 7 → issue + 7 days). Manual override allowed; a mismatch warning is shown if terms and due date disagree. *(Fixes current bug.)*
- **FR-DOC-4 [M]** Default notes/terms text is **document-type-aware**: invoices never contain receipt language and vice versa. All default copy is proofread and locale-consistent. *(Fixes "receipt/recieved" bug.)*
- **FR-DOC-5 [M]** One currency formatter used across PDF, email, WhatsApp, SMS, and hosted page (e.g., $6,200.00 everywhere). Currency symbol per business profile.
- **FR-DOC-6 [M]** Quantity labels pluralize correctly (1 session / 4 sessions).
- **FR-DOC-7 [M]** Duplicate any past document as the starting point for a new one.
- **FR-DOC-8 [S]** Convert: Quote → Invoice (one tap, number sequence continues); Invoice → Receipt on payment (see PAY module).
- **FR-DOC-9 [S]** Draft autosave (local for guests, server for accounts).
- **FR-DOC-10 [C]** Multi-currency per document; multi-language templates (EN/FR/SW).

### 3.4 Module: Branding Engine (BRAND)
- **FR-BRAND-1 [M]** Logo upload (PNG/JPG); automatic dominant-color extraction applied to PDF accents, email header card, and hosted page. *(Existing strength - keep.)*
- **FR-BRAND-2 [M]** Manual color override.
- **FR-BRAND-3 [S]** 3–5 PDF layout templates.
- **FR-BRAND-4 [M]** Free tier keeps a small, tasteful "Created with KraaFo" footer **that is a hyperlink** on PDF, email, and hosted page. Removing it is a future paid feature.

### 3.5 Module: Delivery (DEL)
- **FR-DEL-1 [M]** Two send modes: **Bundle** (email + WhatsApp + SMS in one action; default) and **Per-channel** (pick channels individually). *(Existing - keep.)*
- **FR-DEL-2 [M]** Email: branded summary card + attached PDF + hosted-page button. Sent from KraaFo domain with sender's business name as display name; **Reply-To = sender's email**.
- **FR-DEL-3 [M]** WhatsApp/SMS messages are **short (≤4 lines)**: business name, doc number, amount, due date, and the **hosted-page link**. No "check your email" dead ends.
- **FR-DEL-4 [M]** All footers/taglines ("via KraaFo") link to kraafo.com with UTM tags.
- **FR-DEL-5 [M]** Per-account send quotas on free tier (e.g., X SMS/month) with clear in-app meter; email effectively unmetered within abuse limits.
- **FR-DEL-6 [M]** Delivery status per channel per send: queued → sent → delivered → failed, visible to the sender.
- **FR-DEL-7 [S]** Resend / send-to-additional-channel from document history.

### 3.6 Module: Hosted Document Pages (HOST) - *the product's centerpiece*
- **FR-HOST-1 [M]** Every sent document gets a unique, unguessable URL: `kraafo.com/i/{token}` (invoice), `/q/`, `/r/`.
- **FR-HOST-2 [M]** Page shows the full document (mobile-optimized HTML, not just embedded PDF), Download PDF button, sender contact actions (email, call, WhatsApp reply).
- **FR-HOST-3 [M]** First view fires a **view event** (see TRACK). Page requires no login for the recipient.
- **FR-HOST-4 [M]** Footer CTA: "Send professional invoices like this - free" → kraafo.com. *(This is the growth loop.)*
- **FR-HOST-5 [M]** Open Graph tags so the link previews beautifully in WhatsApp (business name, amount, KraaFo brand).
- **FR-HOST-6 [P2]** **Pay Now** button on the hosted page (mobile money / bank / card). This page is where monetization lives.
- **FR-HOST-7 [S]** Sender can void/expire a link.

### 3.7 Module: Tracking & Notifications (TRACK)
- **FR-TRACK-1 [M]** Record document events: sent, delivered, **viewed** (first + count), downloaded, paid.
- **FR-TRACK-2 [M]** Notify the sender on first view: "Bright Future Solutions viewed Invoice INV-0019 ✓" via email and (opt-in) WhatsApp. *(Primary retention hook.)*
- **FR-TRACK-3 [M]** Document list shows status chips: Draft / Sent / Viewed / Overdue / Paid.
- **FR-TRACK-4 [S]** Weekly digest email: outstanding total, overdue list, "nudge" shortcuts.

### 3.8 Module: Reminders (REM)
- **FR-REM-1 [M]** One-tap manual reminder from any unpaid invoice: pre-written polite message re-sent to chosen channels with the hosted link.
- **FR-REM-2 [S]** Automatic reminder schedule (opt-in per invoice): e.g., 3 days before due, on due date, 3/7 days after. Sender can edit copy.
- **FR-REM-3 [M]** Overdue detection: any unpaid invoice past due date is flagged and surfaced.

### 3.9 Module: Payments & Receipts (PAY)
- **FR-PAY-1 [M]** Sender can **mark an invoice paid** (full/partial; date, method, reference).
- **FR-PAY-2 [M]** On paid-in-full, offer one-tap **auto-generated receipt** referencing the invoice, sendable through the same delivery bundle. *(Closes the loop; deepens habit.)*
- **FR-PAY-3 [P2]** Payment collection on hosted page: mobile money (M-Pesa / MTN MoMo per market), bank transfer instructions, card via PSP (e.g., Paystack/Flutterwave-class provider). Webhook marks invoice paid automatically → auto-receipt.
- **FR-PAY-4 [P2]** KraaFo fee model: small % or flat fee per collected payment. Free everywhere else.

### 3.10 Module: History & Dashboard (HIST)
- **FR-HIST-1 [M]** Document list with search/filter by client, status, date, type.
- **FR-HIST-2 [M]** Simple money view: total outstanding, total overdue, collected this month.
- **FR-HIST-3 [C]** CSV export.

### 3.11 Module: Marketing Site & SEO (SEO)
- **FR-SEO-1 [M]** All public pages (home, templates, guides, hosted docs) are **server-rendered / prerendered**: full content visible to any HTTP client without JavaScript.
- **FR-SEO-2 [M]** Bot management **allowlists verified crawlers** (Googlebot, Bingbot) and link-preview bots (WhatsApp, Facebook, Twitter); blocks abusive bots only.
- **FR-SEO-3 [M]** sitemap.xml, robots.txt, canonical URLs; registered in Google Search Console **and Bing Webmaster Tools**.
- **FR-SEO-4 [M]** Structured data: Organization, WebApplication (price: 0), FAQPage.
- **FR-SEO-5 [M]** Meta/OG copy is truthful to the product (no-signup claim matches FR-AUTH-1/2 behavior).
- **FR-SEO-6 [S]** Programmatic page system: template-driven landing pages by trade and by country keyword, publishable from a content sheet.

### 3.12 Module: Admin Console (ADMIN)
- **FR-ADMIN-1 [M]** Funnel metrics replace vanity metrics: visitors → invoice_started → invoice_completed → sent → viewed → paid; weekly repeat senders. *(Session duration and bounce demoted.)*
- **FR-ADMIN-2 [M]** Delivery health: per-channel success rates, bounce/complaint rates, quota consumption.
- **FR-ADMIN-3 [M]** Abuse controls: rate limits, content screening on send (anti-phishing), user suspension.

## 4. Non-Functional Requirements

### 4.1 Performance
- **NFR-PERF-1** First invoice creatable in **<60 seconds** by a new user on a mid-range phone (the brand promise; measured, not assumed).
- **NFR-PERF-2** Landing page LCP <2.5 s on 3G-class connection; hosted document page <2 s.
- **NFR-PERF-3** PDF generation <3 s p95.

### 4.2 Deliverability (existential)
- **NFR-DEL-1** SPF, DKIM, DMARC configured and monitored on the sending domain.
- **NFR-DEL-2** Marketing/lifecycle email uses a **separate subdomain or domain** from transactional invoice email.
- **NFR-DEL-3** Complaint rate <0.1%; bounce handling and suppression lists automatic.

### 4.3 Security & Anti-abuse
- **NFR-SEC-1** Hosted-page tokens ≥128-bit random; no sequential IDs.
- **NFR-SEC-2** Send actions gated by verified account + rate limits + heuristic screening (invoice tools are phishing vectors; this protects domain reputation and users).
- **NFR-SEC-3** Data encrypted in transit and at rest; passwords hashed (argon2/bcrypt).
- **NFR-SEC-4** Recipient personal data minimized; deletion on request (align with Ghana DPA / Kenya DPA / GDPR-style rights).

### 4.4 Reliability & Availability
- **NFR-REL-1** 99.5%+ availability for hosted pages (recipients click these from payment conversations).
- **NFR-REL-2** Delivery jobs queued with retry + dead-letter; no silent send failures.

### 4.5 Usability & Localization
- **NFR-USE-1** Fully usable one-handed on a 360-px-wide screen.
- **NFR-USE-2** Currency, date format, and phone formats per market; copy in clear plain English v1; FR/SW ready.
- **NFR-USE-3** All user-visible default copy passes proofreading review before release.

## 6. Data Model (overview)
- **User** (id, email, verified, created_at)
- **Business** (id, user_id, name, logo_url, brand_color, contact fields, currency, payment_instructions, default_terms, doc_number_counters)

- **Event** (id, document_id, kind[sent|delivered|viewed|downloaded|reminded|paid], meta, created_at)
- **Payment** (id, document_id, amount, method, reference, paid_at)

## 9. Acceptance Criteria (samples)
- **AC-1 (crawlability):** `curl https://kraafo.com` returns HTML containing the H1 and ≥300 words of body copy. Google Search Console shows homepage indexed.
- **AC-2 (send gate):** A guest completes and downloads an invoice PDF with no account; tapping Send prompts signup; after email verification, the send completes without rebuilding the document.
- **AC-3 (dead-end fix):** A WhatsApp delivery contains ≤4 lines and a working hosted-page link whose preview shows business name and amount.
- **AC-4 (loop):** Recipient opens hosted page → sender receives "viewed" notification within 60 s → hosted page footer CTA click lands on kraafo.com with UTM recorded.
- **AC-5 (copy integrity):** No invoice can render the word "receipt" in default notes; terms Net-N always matches (or warns about) the due date; totals formatted identically across all five surfaces.
